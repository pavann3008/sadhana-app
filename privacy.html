1. Introduction

Sadhana ("the App", "we", "our") is a Hindu spiritual companion application for Android and iOS. We provide daily panchang, japa counter, Bhagavad Gita shlokas, mantras, and personalized spiritual guidance.

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using the App you agree to the terms of this Policy.

This policy complies with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and, to the extent applicable, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

4. Data Storage and Security

4.1 On-device storage

All japa counts, streaks, favourites, and panchang cache are stored only on your device in an encrypted SQLite database. This data does not leave your device unless you link an account.

4.2 Cloud storage (Supabase)

If you link an email address or use the App while online, the following data is stored in our Supabase database (hosted on AWS in the us-east-1 region):

  • Your anonymous session ID (or email-linked user ID)
  • Preferences: language, deity, region, routine times, spiritual goals
  • Personal profile fields you have explicitly entered: name, gender, phone, birth date, birth time
  • Earned badges and streak count

All data transmitted is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.2+. Data at rest is encrypted using AES-256.

4.3 Row-level security

Our Supabase database enforces Row Level Security (RLS): each user can only read and write their own profile row. No user can access another user's data.

7. Your Rights

7.1 Right to access

You can view all personal information you have provided by opening the Profile tab in the App.

7.2 Right to correction

You can update your name, gender, phone, birth date, birth time, language, deity, and region at any time from the Profile tab.

7.3 Right to deletion (Right to be forgotten)

You may request complete deletion of your account and all associated data by:

  1. Going to Profile → Account → Delete account (in-app option), or
  2. Emailing support@sadhana.app with subject line "Data deletion request"

We will delete all data from our Supabase database within 30 days and send a confirmation.

7.4 Right to data portability

You may request a copy of your stored profile data by emailing support@sadhana.app. We will provide it in JSON format within 30 days.

7.5 Right to withdraw consent

If you have linked an email address, you may sign out at any time from Profile → Account → Sign out. Notifications can be disabled from Profile → Notifications or your device Settings.

7.6 Right to lodge a complaint

If you are in the EU/UK, you may lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. If you are in India, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India once constituted under the DPDP Act.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Contact us at support@sadhana.app if you believe a child under 13 has provided us personal information.

9. Push Notifications

The App may request permission to send local push notifications for a daily shloka, an evening aarti reminder, and festival alerts. These notifications are scheduled locally on your device — no remote push notification server is involved (no FCM or APNs server call is made for scheduling). You can disable them at any time from your device Settings or from Profile → Notifications.
