Getting started

Set up Mindful Pause in under a minute

Four quick steps and every distracting tap gets a five-second breath first.

1

Open Mindful Pause

From the Sadhana home screen, tap the Mindful Pause card to enter setup.

2

Choose your apps

Pick up to three apps that pull your attention most — Instagram, YouTube, WhatsApp or others.

3

Grant accessibility access

Android needs this one-time permission so Sadhana can detect when a chosen app opens — nothing is read or recorded.

4

Set your pause length

Keep the default five seconds or adjust it — then every launch of a chosen app opens with a shloka first.

FAQ

Common questions

Is Sadhana free to use?

Yes — every core feature, including Panchang, mantras, the japa counter and Mindful Pause, is free. No ads, no paywalls.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Panchang, mantras, the japa counter and Mindful Pause all run fully on-device — no connection required.

Is my data private?

Sadhana collects no data. There are no ad SDKs, no background tracking and no third-party sharing. Everything stays on your phone; an optional email sign-in only backs up your data.

Do I need to create an account?

No account is required for any feature. Sign-in is optional, only for backup and restore.

Which traditions does Sadhana support?

Sadhana respects all major sampradayas equally — Vaishnavism, Shaivism, Shaktism, Smarthism and Madhwa — and adapts to your chosen Ishta Devata.
